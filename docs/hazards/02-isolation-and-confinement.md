## 3. Additional Researched Datasets, Standards & Information Fields

To make Vitalis's deterministic numerical layer and simulation scenarios genuinely auditable (rather than arbitrary scores), the following external datasets, instruments, and engineering standards were researched and mapped:

### A. NASA Human Research Roadmap (HRR) Formal Risks
1. **BMed Risk** (*Risk of Adverse Cognitive or Behavioral Conditions and Psychiatric Disorders*): Tracks individual depression, anxiety, cognitive decline, and adjustment issues in Isolated, Confined, and Extreme (ICE) environments.
2. **Team Risk** (*Risk of Performance and Behavioral Health Decrements Due to Inadequate Cooperation, Coordination, Communication, and Psychosocial Adaptation within a Team*): Tracks crew cohesion, leadership/communication breakdown under comms lag, and interpersonal tension.
3. **Sleep Risk** (*Risk of Performance Decrements and Adverse Health Outcomes Resulting from Sleep Loss, Circadian Desynchronization, and Work Overload*): Tracks cumulative sleep debt, circadian misalignment, and fatigue-induced operational error.

### B. NASA-STD-3001 (Vol. 2) Habitability & Environmental Thresholds
Isolation stress is tightly coupled to physical habitat constraints regulated by **NASA-STD-3001 Vol. 2**:
- **Acoustic Limits:** Continuous noise must not exceed **NC-50 (~58 dBA)** in work areas and **NC-40 (~49 dBA)** in sleep quarters for missions **>30 days** (hearing protection cannot be used to claim compliance). Intermittent/impulse noise spikes during sleep are tightly bounded above ambient baseline to prevent sleep fragmentation.
- **Privacy & Habitable Volume:** Missions **>30 days** mandate dedicated **individual privacy quarters** and controlled personal sleep environments (temperature, ventilation, localized lighting) to curb crowding stress and emotional exhaustion.
- **Dynamic Circadian Lighting:** Tunable solid-state lighting schedules—blue-enriched white light during operational wake windows for alertness, and dim, red-shifted spectra pre-sleep to protect melatonin onset.

### C. Validated Neurobehavioral & Psychophysiological Benchmarks
- **NASA *Cognition* 10-Test Battery & PVT-B (Basner, Dinges, Gur):** Standardised iPad/computerized battery (12–25 min full; 1–3 min per subtest, 15 alternate stimulus forms to prevent practice effects). Includes **Psychomotor Vigilance Test (PVT-B)** for millisecond reaction time & attentional lapses, **Digit Symbol Substitution (DSST)**, **Fractal 2-Back (F2B)** working memory, **Emotion Recognition (ERT)**, and **Balloon Analog Risk Test (BART)** for impulse/risk propensity.
- **ISS Sleep Deficit Benchmark (Barger et al., 2014):** Across ISS missions, **8.5 hours** of sleep is scheduled, yet astronauts average only **6.09 hours/night** (Shuttle: **5.96 hours/night**), with **75% of ISS crew** using sleep medication at least once.
- **Mars500 520-Day Isolation Dataset (Basner et al., 2014, *PLOS ONE*):**
  - Demonstrated **protracted crew hypokinesis** (progressive decline in daytime physical activity and rise in sedentary rest).
  - Identified strong **inter-individual vulnerability**: some crew adapted smoothly while others suffered chronic sleep-onset insomnia, circadian free-running/drift, elevated POMS/BDI distress, and PVT vigilance lapses.
  - Documented the **"Third-Quarter Phenomenon"**: a pronounced dip in mood, motivation, and cohesion past mission midpoint when novelty fades and return remains distant.
- **CHAPEA & HERA Behavioral Suites:** Tracks **APEX** (*Assessment of Psychology in Extreme Environments*) mood/stress/conflict surveys, actigraphy + lux exposure, and team problem-solving under **up to 44-minute round-trip communication delays** (22 min each way) and resource constraints.
- **Concordia (Antarctica) & HI-SEAS:** Validates **Isolation Standard Measures (ISM)**—PANAS, PSS-10 (Perceived Stress Scale), actigraphy, salivary cortisol/melatonin, and "psychological hibernation" coping patterns under winter-over darkness and monotony.
- **NASA OSDR & NLSP Biomarker Fields:** *Inspiration4* (`OSD-569`, `OSD-571`, `OSD-575`, `OSD-656`) and HERA psycho-physiological studies establish measurable stress-response fields—**salivary/plasma cortisol**, **melatonin**, **heart rate variability (HRV)**, **BDNF**, and **immune/inflammatory cytokines**—which interact with sleep deprivation and isolation stress.

### B. Deterministic Numerical Layer: Rules, Thresholds & Time-to-Action
Rather than vague wellness scores, Hazard 2 alerts compute transparent **debuffs** and **Estimated Time of Action (ETA)** windows from documented benchmarks:

1. **Cumulative Sleep Debt & Vigilance Decay (Barger 2014 + PVT-B):**
   - **Baseline:** 8.5h scheduled vs. 6.09h ISS historical average.
   - **Trigger:** Rolling 3-day `sleep_duration_h < 6.5h` OR `pvt_lapses_count` exceeding personal baseline by `>= +2 SD`.
   - **Horizon & ETA:** Prolonged Alert (24–72h) escalating to Immediate Alert (**ETA: 2–6 hours before scheduled EVA/critical task**) if acute vigilance impairment risks operational error.
2. **Martian Sol & Circadian Drift (CHAPEA + NASA-STD-3001 Lighting):**
   - **Trigger:** `circadian_phase_shift_min >= 39 min/day` (uncompensated $24\text{h }39\text{m}$ Martian sol drift) or pre-sleep blue-light exposure mismatch (`ambient_lux` high within 2h of sleep).
   - **Horizon & ETA:** Prolonged Alert (**ETA: 3–5 sols** until severe circadian desynchronization & melatonin suppression).
3. **Acoustic Sleep Disruption Debuff (NASA-STD-3001 Vol. 2):**
   - **Trigger:** Sleep-quarter continuous noise `> NC-40 (~49 dBA)` on missions `>30 days`, or repeated impulse spikes (`> +10 dB` over ambient) during crew sleep window.
   - **Cross-Crew Attribution:** Automatically flags as a **Team-Wide Environmental Debuff** rather than individual insomnia when `>= 2` crew quarters show elevated NC levels.
4. **Confinement Monotony, Hypokinesis & Third-Quarter Dip (Mars500 + HERA):**
   - **Trigger:** `mission_day_pct` between `50%–75%` + 7-day downward slope in `daytime_activity_counts` (hypokinesis) + rising `subjective_fatigue` or declining HRV.
   - **Horizon & ETA:** Prolonged Alert (**ETA: 7–14 days** to prevent entrenched mood/motivation slump and team friction).
5. **Autonomy & Comms-Lag Stress (CHAPEA + SIRIUS):**
   - **Trigger:** `comms_rtt_delay_min >= 10 min` (up to 44 min Mars max) paired with elevated workload (`> scheduled duty hours`) and lowered crew coordination check-in score.

### C. Privacy-Preserving Commander Readiness Matrix (Buffs vs. Debuffs)
In alignment with [OUTLINE.md](file:///Users/alex/Programming/projects/nasa-space-apps-2026/docs/OUTLINE.md), the system enforces a strict **privacy firewall**:

| Strictly Private (Individual Crew View Only) | Aggregated / Shared (Commander Readiness Matrix) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Raw journal entries (`3LD3od5E64U`), personal mood/depression scores (BDI-II/PANAS), individual medication/hypnotic usage, personal relationship notes. | **Crew-Wide Sleep Readiness Index** (anonymized aggregate % below 6.5h threshold), **Shared Habitat Acoustic/Lighting Debuffs** (NC-40 breach), **Comms-Delay Autonomy Strain**, **Third-Quarter Mission Risk Banner**, and **Non-Clinical Duty-Readiness Status** (Green / Amber / Red) without clinical text. |

- **Team-Wide Buffs (Protective Factors):**
  - *Synchronized Sleep & Lighting Hygiene Buff:* `>= 75%` crew achieving `>= 7.0h` sleep with compliant pre-sleep dim/red lighting.
  - *Biophilia / Space Garden Buff:* Active *Veggie*/plant-care rotation completed across crew (sensory enrichment & mood stabilization).
  - *CONNECT Cohesion Buff:* Weekly shared crew meal/briefing + async family networking logged under high-delay periods.
  - *Anti-Hypokinesis Exercise Buff:* Crew-wide resistance/aerobic compliance preventing Mars500-style sedentary drift.

### D. Example Dual-Horizon Alerts & 1–2 Resource-Constrained Solutions
Every alert pairs an **Estimated Time of Action** with **1–2 concrete solutions** filtered by time and onboard inventory:

- **Scenario 1 — Shared Acoustic & Sleep-Debt Debuff (Prolonged -> Immediate):**
  - *Detection:* 3 of 4 crew show `sleep_duration_h` dropping from `7.2h` to `5.8h` over 4 nights; habitat telemetry logs `54 dBA` (`> NC-40`) in Crew Quarters B & C from a degraded ECLSS fan.
  - *Commander View:* "Team Sleep Debt Debuff (-18% readiness) linked to Crew Quarters acoustic breach (54 dBA vs. NC-40 limit). **ETA: 18h** before next EVA safety threshold." (Hides which crewmember logged high anxiety or used sleep aids.)
  - *1–2 Solutions:*
    1. **Immediate (15 min):** Inspect/re-seat ECLSS fan muffler in Zone B/C *(Tools: onboard hex kit — In Stock)*; issue crew foam earplugs/ANC headsets from Med Kit Bay 2 *(Inventory: 12 pairs)* as temporary bridge.
    2. **Schedule (Tonight):** Shift non-critical maintenance by 60 min to restore an 8.0h protected sleep window.

- **Scenario 2 — Third-Quarter Monotony & Hypokinesis Drift (Prolonged):**
  - *Detection:* Mission Day 210/365 (58% mark); actigraphy shows `-22%` daytime movement over 10 days alongside rising PVT-B reaction times (`+35 ms` over baseline).
  - *Individual View:* "Vigilance & Energy Drift Detected (**ETA: 7 days** to sustained fatigue). You're entering the classic mission third-quarter monotony window."
  - *1–2 Solutions (CONNECT + Veggie-grounded):*
    1. **10-Min Sensory Reset (Now):** Complete today's *Veggie* plant-module check & misting *(Biophilia / sensory countermeasure)* or a 10-min private audio/journal reflection.
    2. **3-Min Self-Check (Before Shift):** Run a 3-min **PVT-B** check to verify alertness before precision tasks; schedule 30-min ARED/cycle session with crewmate *(Anti-hypokinesis + Community)*.

- **Scenario 3 — Re-Entry / Post-Isolation Transition Mode (Phase-Aware UI):**
  - *Detection:* Mission phase switches to `POST_ISOLATION_REENTRY` (grounded in NASA *Five Tips on Emerging from Isolation*).
  - *System Behavior:* Automatically throttles non-critical alerts, caps action cards to **1 step** to prevent decision fatigue, lowers UI notification cadence to prevent sensory overload, and prompts gradual routine/social pacing.

### E. Earth-Analog Transferability
Every Hazard 2 model rule above maps cleanly without structural changes to isolated terrestrial teams lacking on-site physicians:
- **Antarctic Winter-Over Stations (e.g., Concordia):** Polar night circadian drift, third-quarter morale dip, shared HVAC noise, and winter-over re-entry overload.
- **Submarines & Offshore Rigs:** Strict NC-40/50 acoustic confinement, shift-work sleep debt, high operational stakes, and delayed medevac.
- **Remote Field & Disaster Response Teams:** Rapid team-wide debuff detection and privacy-preserving leader readiness visibility.
